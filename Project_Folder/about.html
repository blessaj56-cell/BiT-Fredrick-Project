Our Company: The Story Behind the Star

Mercedes-Benz traces its roots to 1886, when Karl Benz patented the Benz Patent-Motorwagen, widely regarded as the first practical automobile. In the same period, Gottlieb Daimler and Wilhelm Maybach were building motorised vehicles of their own.

Mercedes-Benz Models: From Everyday Saloons to Icons.

Mercedes-Benz sells one of the broadest ranges in the premium car market,
including compact cars (A-Class, CLA, GLA, GLB), executive saloons (C-Class, E-Class),
the flagship S-Class and Mercedes-Maybach, SUVs (GLC, GLE, GLS, G-Class), AMG performance models,
electric EQ vehicles and vans such as the Sprinter. In 2025 the company sold around 1.8 million passenger cars worldwide,
with the C-Class, E-Class and A-Class families the biggest sellers, while the G-Class
had its best year ever, selling roughly 48,000 to 50,000 units, up about 23%.

Iconic Mercedes-Benz Models

Mercedes-Benz EQC 400 4MATIC

he EQC is a fully electric luxury SUV powered by two electric motors, producing 408 hp and 760 Nm of torque. It has an 80 kWh battery, an all-wheel-drive system, a top speed of 180 km/h and accelerates from 0–100 km/h in 5.1 seconds.

Mercedes-Benz G-Class (G 500)

The Mercedes-Benz G-Class, popularly known as the G-Wagon, is a luxury off-road SUV featuring a 3.0-litre inline-six turbocharged petrol engine with mild-hybrid technology (2024 model). It produces approximately 449 hp and 560 Nm of torque, paired with a 9-speed automatic transmission and permanent all-wheel drive. It accelerates from 0–100 km/h in approximately 5.4 seconds and has a top speed of 210 km/h.

2024 Mercedes-AMG GT 63 4MATIC+

This high-performance sports car features a 4.0-litre twin-turbocharged V8 petrol engine, producing 585 PS and 800 Nm of torque. It has a 9-speed automatic transmission, all-wheel drive, a top speed of 315 km/h and accelerates from 0–100 km/h in just 3.2 seconds.
